Who We Are

About Siana Sidai ee Maa CBO

Founded in 2015 and formally registered in 2024, Siana Sidai ee Maa is a dynamic, not-for-profit community-based organisation transforming lives in Kajiado Central Sub County, Kenya.

Co-founded by Peter Leposo, Agnes Naserian, Juliet Sita, Richard Ogega, Peter Kasaine, Frank Okemwa, and Caroline Mumbi, the CBO has successfully championed poverty alleviation and livelihood improvement for both people and livestock, earning the trust of over 51% of local households.

Looking ahead, the organisation is poised to expand its impact through strategic local and international partnerships, scaling up community-led projects, and securing a sustainable future for the communities it serves.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

🔭 Our Vision

Building a thriving, self-reliant community where every individual and household enjoys dignity, improved quality of life, and sustainable livelihoods, with reliable access to essential resources and inclusive development that supports both people and animals for a healthy and productive future.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower the community by improving access to essential livelihood resources and services, driving poverty reduction, and enhancing overall quality of life. Through sustainable development programs in health, education, and economic opportunities, we foster resilience, equity, and holistic well-being, integrating both human and animal welfare in all our interventions.

What We Do

Our Services

Siana Sidai ee Maa delivers a range of community-driven services designed to uplift livelihoods and enhance resilience in Kajiado Central Sub County.

🌾

Livelihood Support Projects

Improving access to basic resources and income-generating activities for both households and livestock across the community.

💰

Poverty Alleviation Programs

Targeted interventions that address economic vulnerability and promote long-term self-sufficiency among community members.

🤝

Community Empowerment & Sensitization

Training and awareness sessions on rights, resources, and sustainable practices that build capacity and informed citizenship.

🌍

Climate Change Protection

Initiatives to mitigate environmental degradation and help the community adapt to changing weather patterns and climate threats.

💧

Water Services

Facilitating access to clean and reliable water for domestic use and livestock, a critical need in the semi-arid Kajiado region.

🏛️

Partnership-Driven Service Delivery

Collaborating with Kajiado County Government, donors, and international partners to scale up essential community services.

How We Work

Our Process

Our work is grounded in democratic, transparent, and community-led principles from planning to implementation.

1

Needs Assessment

Community members voice their priorities in general meetings, with a mandate from 51%+ of households ensuring every decision reflects the community's true needs.

2

Democratic Planning

Elected leaders prepare budgets and work plans, which are reviewed and approved at Annual General Meetings for full community ownership.

3

Resource Mobilisation

Funds are raised via member contributions, donations, and strategic partnerships, banked transparently with full accountability to members.

4

Implementation & Accountability

Services are delivered equitably across the community, with detailed financial and progress reports shared at every AGM.

Our Impact

Achievements

Since 2015, Siana Sidai ee Maa has delivered tangible, life-changing results across Kajiado Central Sub County.

1

Fed over 50 families in Kajiado Central Sub County through targeted food relief programmes.

2

Empowered street families in Kajiado and Kitengela Towns through engagement and rehabilitation initiatives.

3

Introduced beekeeping as an alternative livelihood, promoting new family income methods within the Maasai community.

4

Planted trees in partnership with institutions to enhance environmental conservation and climate action.

5

Engaged students in schools through outreach, awareness, and capacity-building programmes at Oloyiankalani Girls Secondary School and others.

6

Partnered with local investors to drive community empowerment and sustainable development projects.
